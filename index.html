HOW TO PARTICIPATE · PARTICIPATION BRACKETS

One competition, three fields of entry.

Every bracket attempts the same challenge categories and is ranked on its own leaderboard, so a first-year student is never measured against a career researcher or a serving investigator.

BRACKET I

Open

Students

UG & PG · recognised institutions

The proving ground for the next generation of defenders, and where NFSU meets emerging cyber talent from across India.

  • Teams of 1–3 with a valid institutional ID
  • Registration fee: ₹350 (inclusive of GST)
  • Dedicated student leaderboard and prizes
  • Participation rewards for every registered team

BRACKET II

Open

Professionals

Industry · researchers · red teams

For working security professionals and independent researchers seeking a recognised national benchmark against their peers.

  • Separate professional leaderboard
  • Category-wise specialist recognition
  • Access to the hiring partners on campus

BRACKET III

By Nominations

Law Enforcement

Cyber cells · forensic labs · agencies

A closed bracket for serving personnel from police cyber units, state forensic laboratories and central agencies, administered with operational discretion.

  • Anonymised or unit-code participation
  • Challenges mapped to investigative skills
  • Capability benchmarking, not public ranking

FAIR PLAY

Competition rules.

These general rules apply to fair participation. Terms and conditions apply. Participation is subject to the organizers’ eligibility requirements and decisions. Detailed rules will be published soon.

01 · Play within scope

Attack only the challenge systems and resources explicitly designated by the organizers. Do not target the competition platform, infrastructure, other teams, or third-party systems.

02 · Work with your team

Collaborate only with your registered teammates. Do not share flags, solutions, accounts, or challenge access with other teams during the competition.

03 · Keep the event available

Do not use denial-of-service attacks, disruptive scans, excessive automated requests, or actions that affect other participants or the availability of services.

04 · Submit your own work

Submit flags obtained by your team through the intended challenge environment. Do not use leaked flags or interfere with another team's progress.

05 · Report problems privately

Report unintended vulnerabilities, infrastructure issues, or suspected rule violations to the organizers through the official support channel. Do not disclose them publicly during the event.

06 · Follow organizer decisions

Follow official announcements and instructions. Organizers may investigate violations, adjust challenges or scores, and disqualify teams for unfair conduct. Teams may have 1–3 members. Eligibility, scoring, tie-breaks, and prize conditions will be announced separately.

COMPETITION INFORMATION

Frequently asked questions.

What is KAALCHAKRA CTF?+

A national level Capture The Flag competition organized by National Forensic Sciences University, Goa, in collaboration with TH3_RANG3RS. Solve cybersecurity challenges, uncover hidden flags, and earn your place on the leaderboard.

When will the competition take place?+

Edition 2.0 dates have not been announced yet. The official schedule will be published here and on our community channels.

How can I register?+

Registration is not open yet. Teams may have 1–3 members. The registration link, eligibility, and rules will be released once confirmed. Join the Discord community to follow announcements.

Do I need to be an experienced player?+

Curiosity is a good place to start. Practice web security, cryptography, forensics, and reverse engineering to prepare. Official eligibility and difficulty details will accompany the 2.0 rules.

What prizes are available?+

KAALCHAKRA 2.0 has a total cash prize pool of ₹3,50,000. The prize breakdown will be announced soon.
