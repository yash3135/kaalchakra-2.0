How it went

From qualifier to podium

  1. 28 March 2026

    Online qualifier

    600+ participants, 24 hours of jeopardy-style challenges across web, cryptography, forensics, reversing, OSINT, AI/ML and misc.

  2. Right after

    Write-up verification

    Top teams had 8 hours to submit write-ups. Only verified teams moved on.

  3. 11 April 2026

    Offline final at NFSU Goa Campus

    25 teams, 75 finalists, competing on campus from 9 AM to 6 PM.

  4. 11 April 2026

    Prize ceremony

    The top three teams received cheques and partner perks on stage.
